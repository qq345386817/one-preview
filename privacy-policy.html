Privacy Policy

OnePreview is designed for local file preview and does not collect personal information.

Download on the App Store

Information collection

OnePreview does not require an account and does not send analytics, personal information, keystrokes, file names, file paths or file contents. Limited performance diagnostics stay local and are shared only if you choose to attach them to a feedback email.

File handling

Files are opened through Apple system preview mechanisms and rendered locally on your device.

Network access

The preview renderer may request remote images referenced by a document. Markdown rendering libraries are bundled with OnePreview and do not require a CDN.

Third parties

We do not sell, share or upload your documents to third parties.

Contact
